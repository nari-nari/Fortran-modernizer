# Project Goal

Modernize the legacy Fortran lubrication-analysis codebase into a
maintainable, verifiable, and extensible implementation.

The resulting codebase should be easy to understand, test, maintain,
and extend, with clear variable responsibilities and dependencies.

The long-term goal is to support future Python-based development,
including potential use of Firedrake, automatic differentiation,
adjoint sensitivity analysis, and topology optimization.

Do not modify files under `legacy/original/`.
